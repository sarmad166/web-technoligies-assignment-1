Welcome to TechFest

TechFest 2026 is our college’s annual technology festival, bringing together students, teachers and industry guests. Expect live demos, hands-on workshops and a student project showcase.

Whether you are into web design, robotics, or data, there is a session for you. Entry is free for students (ID required).
Seats are limited, so register early!

We follow the W3C accessibility guidelines for all event materials.

Key Facts

  • Dates: 14–15 November 2026
  • Venue: Innovation Building, Main Campus
  • Tickets: Free for students, $10 for guests
  • Tracks: Web, AI, Robotics, Design, Cybersecurity

How to Register

  1. Read the schedule and pick the sessions you like.
  2. Keep your student ID or CV ready as a PDF or image.
  3. Open the registration form and complete every required field.
